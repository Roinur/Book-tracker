# Privacy

Book Tracker stores the library, reading sessions, notes, goals and active timer state locally. It has no account service or application analytics backend. Android system backup may include local app data according to device settings.

Book and cover lookups send search terms or ISBNs to Open Library, Google Books and/or Libris. Optional Google Images search loads Google's website in an in-app browser, where Google and any visited websites may use cookies and process browsing activity under their own policies. Downloading covers contacts the image provider. Camera access is used for barcode scanning.

ML Kit processes barcode images on the device. Google documents that the SDK may contact its servers and send performance/utilization metrics: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

JSON exports contain personal reading data and notes. Choose where to store and share them carefully. Original imported data is included for complete preservation.
